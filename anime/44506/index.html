南海奇皇

あらすじ・作品紹介

An ancient God called Ranga awakens on an isolated island in the South Pacific where it has been slumbering forgotten by mankind. Three sisters living in Tokyo&mdash;Minami, Ushio, and Yuuhi Shimabara&mdash;discover they have inherited this Island Kingdom, along with the huge God. When Ranga shows up in Tokyo, it soon becomes a source of wonder and conflict for the three sisters that control Ranga, the people who want to destroy it, and ones who want to use it. <br><br> (Source: Anime News Network)

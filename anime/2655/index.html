あらすじ・作品紹介

In 1992, a pandemic of viral encephalitis shook the world. The medical technology that saved humanity evolved into <i>“Your Forma”</i>—an invasive information device implanted into the brain. In this world where every sight, sound, and even emotion is recorded, investigating major crimes means diving into the collective data, a job reserved for special detectives called “Electronic Investigator.” <br><br> Echika Hieda, a prodigious “Electronic Investigator” assigned at an astonishingly young age, stands isolated by her exceptional talent. Her newly assigned partner is Harold, a blonde, blue-eyed humanoid “Amicus.” Together, this unrivaled duo will expose every hidden secret. <br><br> (Source: "It’s Anime" YouTube Channel)

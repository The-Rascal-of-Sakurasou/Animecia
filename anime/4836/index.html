恋愛日常

あらすじ・作品紹介

Cherry is a quiet boy who loves to make haikus based off of the things he sees. One summer day in a city in the Japanese countryside…<br> <br> At a shopping mall, he meets Smile, a beautiful girl in a mask. Cherry has trouble speaking in anything but haikus, and Smile wears a mask to hide her big, braces-covered front teeth. The two of them get closer as the south wind blows. One day, the two of them learn from Old Man Fujiyama, a master of Haiku who’s started going senile, that he’s searching for a certain record.<br> <br> “If I can hear this record again, I can remember… Something I don’t want to forget, but can’t remember…”<br> <br> Cherry and Smile decide to help. This is the story of their summer, set in a shopping mall…<br> <br> (Source: Hakuhodo DY Music & Pictures)

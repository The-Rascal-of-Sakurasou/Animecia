レモンと話したら

心理

あらすじ・作品紹介

What I say is never what you hear. It’s like the printer misaligning the images little by little. This animation work portrays those communication gaps using various techniques including print, digital treatment and collage. <br><br> 1st year work 2019, Tokyo University of the Arts, Graduate School of Film and New Media, Department of Animation. <br><br> (Source: Geidai Animation)

旅はに 〜京都篇〜

日常

あらすじ・作品紹介

Akari and Nagi come to Kyoto during their first summer holidays after entering high school for a certain purpose. While Akari wants to get there as quickly as possible, Nagi does as much sightseeing as possible to make many memories of their trip. <br><br> Akari urges Nagi to hurry up to their destination but Nagi does otherwise and continues sightseeing leaving Akari no choice but to follow along. While enjoying the sights of Kyoto for the first time, Akari gradually realizes the joys of traveling. <br><br> And then the two finally reach their destination… <br><br> (Source: Official Website, translated) <br><br> Tabi Hani is a crowdfunded project with the theme of traveling through Japan. The first episode "Kyoto-hen" (京都編) was shown to the project's backers at UPLINK Kyoto on April 8th 2023. A second and a third episode were originally planned.

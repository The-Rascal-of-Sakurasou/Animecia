あらすじ・作品紹介

Anthology movie consisting of three original animation segments:<br><br> <b>Q1 - ÆLIENS</b> (エリィアンズ)<br> Earth has entered an era of intergalatic exchange.<br> Amidst swirling prejudice and fear of aliens, Elie, an alien girl, arrives as a space goodwill ambassador. She loves Earth's culture and its often absurd daily life, and has teamed up with a civil servant working at the space agency to search for "friends".<br><br> <b>Q2 - Yoroshiku★Shoujo</b> (夜露死駆★少女)<br> GALs TOKYO, a city where morning never comes.<br> Night after night, girls who have lost their place in reality, known as "Gal P", gather here. Their goal is to become the winner, the "Queen" in a game called "Queen of Butterfly" or "QoB" for short.<br><br> <b>Q3 - Nocturne: Kono Grotesque na Yoru ni</b> (ノクターン～このグロテスクな夜に～)<br> A world after humanity has perished.<br> Traveling in a van are Ayame, a pure-blooded vampire, and three girls who survived by being bitten and "infected" by her. There, they find a sky full of stars that illuminate the night as brightly as day, and fields of glittering golden grass—even a desolate world looks so beautiful through the eyes of a vampire.<br> They enjoy a simple everyday life in this world left behind.<br><br> (Souce: Official Website, translated)

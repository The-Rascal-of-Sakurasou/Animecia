ウィッチウォッチ

コメディファンタジードラマ恋愛日常超自然

あらすじ・作品紹介

Morihito Otogi, a high school student who comes from a lineage of ogres, enjoys a peaceful, ordinary life until his childhood friend, Nico, moves in with him. Nico is a witch-in-training, and chooses Morihito to be her familiar. While Nico is thrilled to reunite with her old friend and crush, Morihito is tasked with the perilous duty to protect her from a foretold calamity. The regular TV broadcast began April 6, 2025.</i>

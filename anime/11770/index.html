あらすじ・作品紹介

Alfred J. Kwak (Dutch, it takes place in The Netherlands) lost his parents, brothers and sisters when he was young. He was raised by a mole. The series covers the life of Alfred from the day he is born. In his life Alfred tries to help all sorts of animals all over the world. The series are meant to entertain children and teach them about life, covering historical events like World War II. <br><br> (Source: AniDB)

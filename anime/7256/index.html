"At this rate, I'll just graduate as a plain background character A!"<br> <br> Minami Aoba was troubled. She had no particular strengths or things she wanted to do. She stumbles upon the nearby golf practice range by chance. Approached by part-time worker Haruka Akane, Minami grasps the golf club—the "utility."<br> <br> And thus, it was the encounter between Minami and golf.<br> <br> Winning is not the only thing in golf! Everything related to golf is enjoyable!<br> <br> Together with the genius golfer Haruka and Ayaka Hoshimi, who aspires to be an influencer, they seek the moment when they can become the "protagonist"! As a super beginner, Minami swings her club once again today.

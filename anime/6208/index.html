特別版 Free!-Take Your Marks-

コメディスポーツ日常

あらすじ・作品紹介

Taking place between the events of <i>Free!: Eternal Summer</i> and <i>Free!: Dive to the Future</i>, <i>Free!: Take Your Marks</i> plays out the graduated seniors' last summer through four different stories before they set sail for the future. <br><br> Unmei no Choice! (Destined Choice!) follows Haruka Nanase and Makoto Tachibana searching for a new apartment for Haruka as he settles into Tokyo. <br><br> Hitou no Cooling Down! (Cooling Down at the Secret Hot Spring!) sees Aiichiro Nitori choose a graduation gift for Rin Matsuoka and Sousuke Yamazaki, while Momotaro Mikoshiba wins tickets to the Anago Hot Springs. Hoping for a trip together, Aiichiro and Momotaro invite Rin and Sousuke. <br><br> Kessoku no Batafurai! (United Butterfly!) shows Rei Ryugazaki, Nagisa Hazuki, and Gou Matsuoka creating a recruitment video to attract new club members for the next school year. <br><br> Tabidachi no Etanaru Buru! (Departing Eternal Blue!) looks into the Iwatobi and Samezuka swim teams' plans for Rin's surprise going-away party, as he will be departing for Australia soon. <br><br> (Source: MAL Rewrite)

神様になった日

コメディSFドラマ恋愛日常

あらすじ・作品紹介

Youta Narukami is a student focused only on his college entrance exams. During his last summer break of high school, he is approached by a girl named Hina who says she is the god of omniscience. She tells him that in thirty days, the world will end. At first Youta doesn't believe her, but after she demonstrates supernatural predictive abilities, he becomes convinced her power is genuine. Hina, meanwhile, decides for some reason to stay at his house, and they begin a tumultuous vacation facing the end of the world together.<br> <br> (Source: Anime News Network)<br> <br> <i>Note: The first episode received an early screening on October 3rd, 2020. The regular TV broadcast started on October 11th, 2020.</i>

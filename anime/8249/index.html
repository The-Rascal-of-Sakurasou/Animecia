アクションドラマ恋愛

あらすじ・作品紹介

A collection of original short episodes based on stories created by light novel authors and illustrators.<br><br> <i>Includes:<br></i> • <i>Shoushin Time Leap<br></i> On her final Christmas in high school, Miri confesses to her classmate Takashi, but he rejects her. In her despair, she clings to a past diary. The diary envelops her in light, and Miri finds herself transported six months in the past. She uses this chance, and repeated time leaps, to try to change her future, and find out the reason why Takashi rejected her confession.<br><br> • <i>Femme Fatale Ikusei Keikaku<br></i> Hiroki is a high school student who is well liked and chubby. After his cute cousin Tsukino is dumped by her ex-boyfriend, Hiroki decides, "I'll become a beautiful woman and make him fall in love with me, and then dump him!" He recruits friends and allies to help him go on a diet and transform into a femme fatale and avenge his cousin.<br><br> • <i>Jack the Reaper<br></i> A man wakes up one day in an interrogation room, with no memory of who he is and why he's there. Before him appears a young female detective. She eventually gets angry with the interrogation and storms out, but an intruder comes in and stabs the man to death. The next moment, he's "back" in the interrogation room.<br><br> • <i>Marie Antoinette ni Tensei Shita node, Zenryoku de Guillotine wo Kaihi Shimasu</i><br> A female otaku from the present day is reincarnated as Marie Antoinette. She refuses to let her future execution play out, so she uses her knowhow as an otaku and her passion to win over the masses — by turning Louis XVI and his brothers into an idol group.

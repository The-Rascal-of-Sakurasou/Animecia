Invertia (meteor storm)… an unprecedented disaster that struck the world during the 20th century.<br> <br> Because of this disaster, numerous cities around the world were destroyed. Having transferred into Seidoukan Academy in order to fulfill his own wish, scholarship student Ayato Amagiri swears he too will fight in this city.<br> <br> Burning souls race…<br> <br> The curtain rises on this school battle spectacle!<br> <br> (Source: Crunchyroll)

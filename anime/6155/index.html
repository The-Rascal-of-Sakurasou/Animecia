Six students are each getting on with their lives, and all of them will visit the nearby convenience store after school.<br><br> Each goes there for different reasons: to eat ice cream after club activities, to get the latest issue of this week's game magazine, to purchase ingredients, to meet friends, or to stock up on their favorite food.

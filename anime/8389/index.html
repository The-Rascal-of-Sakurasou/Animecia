あらすじ・作品紹介

One of the anime projects to commemorate the 20th anniversary of the Precure franchise, this sequel will show characters from <i>Yes! Precure 5</i>, <i>Yes! Precure 5 GoGo!</i> and <i>Futari wa Precure: Splash☆Star</i> as grown-ups.<br><br> Nozomi Yumehara was an ordinary middle school student until she met the mysterious fairy Coco. She became the Precure of Hope, Cure Dream, and fought alongside her friends. Years later, Nozomi and her friends are now adults, busy with work, friends, family, and the stress that adult life brings. As the mysterious Shadows appear and attack humans, Nozomi and her friends embark on a new saga.<br><br> (Source: Anime News Network, Crunchyroll, edited)
